## 1. Purpose and access to the service

Histae is a dating service for adults. It allows users to create a profile, set discovery preferences, view compatible profiles, choose to like or pass on them, and then exchange messages when a mutual choice creates a match. Geographic availability and the features actually available are indicated in the app at the time of use.

Initial access requires a phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS. Users must be at least 18 years old, provide information about themselves and retain control of their phone and sessions. They must not create an account for someone else or circumvent a suspension.

Acceptance of these terms is required to use the service. Acknowledgement of the Privacy Policy is presented separately. Consent to sensitive data processing and to location use is also requested separately, before the features that require it.

## 2. How dating works

Discovery uses the profile, mutual preferences and a recent location when the user has agreed to its use. It does not guarantee profile suggestions, matches or a response from another person. A match is created only after two mutual likes.

The conversation opens for an initial period of 24 hours. An additional 24-hour period then allows both people to decide whether they wish to continue the match. Revealing photos requires separate agreement from both people and does not depend on their decision to continue. These periods and rules reflect the service currently planned; any significant change will be indicated in the app and in a new version of these terms.

Users can block or report another person. Blocking stops the interactions concerned. Reports may be examined by the authorised team.
