## 1. Purpose and access to the service

Histae is strictly restricted to people aged 18 or older. Histae does not allow any person under the age of 18 to register, create a profile or use the service. Falsifying your age, creating an account for a minor or allowing a minor to use your account is prohibited.

Histae is a dating service for adults. It allows users to create a profile, set discovery preferences, view compatible profiles, choose to like or pass on them, and then exchange messages when a mutual choice creates a match. Geographic availability and the features actually available are indicated in the app at the time of use.

Registration uses a French phone number and a one-time SMS code. Verifying the code confirms access to the number, not civil identity. The server refuses to save a profile whose declared birth date indicates an age below 18. This declaration-based check does not certify actual age. There is currently no systematic ID-document, selfie or biometric check, or human approval of every new profile. Users must provide information about themselves, retain control of their sessions and not create an account for someone else or circumvent a suspension.

Acceptance of these terms is required to use the service. Acknowledgement of the Privacy Policy is presented separately. Consent to sensitive data processing and to location use is also requested separately, before the features that require it.

## 2. How dating works

Discovery uses the profile, mutual preferences and a recent location, subject to the required consent. The location is reduced to the centre of an approximate zone of about 5 km, so distance thresholds may be approximate. A technically ready private photo that has not been rejected is required, but is not displayed in the feed. Pause mode removes the profile from new discovery and prevents new likes and matches; existing conversations and subscriptions are retained. Discovery guarantees neither suggestions, matches nor replies.

After two mutual likes, the conversation opens for 24 hours. Sending is then paused during the decision window, which ends 48 hours after match creation; the history remains readable. Two agreements reopen the conversation; a refusal or the absence of two agreements before the deadline ends the match without revealing individual choices. Revealing photos requires two agreements separate from continuation. Either person may withdraw their photo agreement: subsequent reads then hide photos for both people without spending a credit. A replacement becomes the current photo in matches still authorised to share it. An already issued private link may remain usable until expiry, at most 60 seconds for new links, or until object deletion. An image already saved cannot be retrieved remotely.

Users can block or report another person. Blocking stops the interactions concerned. Reports may be examined by the authorised team.

Photos undergo technical conversion without automatic content analysis, face verification, age estimation or comparison with a CSAM hash database. A technically valid photo is not a photo whose content or subject has been verified. Moderation tools allow manual review, content rejection, photo removal and account banning. Users may report a profile or conversation and request review at [support.histae@gmail.com](mailto:support.histae@gmail.com), under the [Acceptable Use Policy](/en/legal/acceptable-use-policy/).

## Continuation credits and optional advertising

Free receives 2 free credits every Monday at 00:00 UTC, without accumulation. Each Free participant reserves their own credit when agreeing to continue; only Free participants are charged after both agreements. A Premium participant spends no credit. Refusal or expiry releases reservations; a credit already spent on a confirmed continuation is not returned when it ends. A validated optional video may grant 1 advertising credit, up to a stock of 3 including reserved credits. Advertising credits expire every Monday at 00:00 UTC; an agreement relying on an expired advertising credit must be renewed before a replacement charge. Credits are internal, non-transferable and have no monetary value. Free allows at most 5 confirmed continuations per person per UTC week. Premium removes this limit and advertising; mutual agreement remains necessary.

## What happens to inactive conversations?

For a confirmed conversation, the inactivity period starts at mutual confirmation and resets only when a new message is sent. After 10 days the conversation is archived but remains accessible; after 23 days a warning is scheduled. At 30 days access closes and maintenance deletes the match, its messages and associated notifications. A new message before the deadline unarchives the conversation without another agreement or additional credit; reading or opening the app does not reset the period. Reports follow their own retention period and may survive match deletion. The same rule applies to Free and Premium.
