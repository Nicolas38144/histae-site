# Histae Law Enforcement Policy

**Published version:** Validation in progress.\
**Document date:** 4 November 2026.\
**Service concerned:** Histae.\
**Publisher's and data controller's legal identity:** Validation in progress.\
**Contact point:** [support.histae@gmail.com](mailto:support.histae@gmail.com)

## 1. Purpose and contact

This document describes the handling of official requests addressed to Histae by competent authorities: legally authorised demands, preservation requests and situations involving serious danger. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy-policy/) and constitutes neither users' consent to blanket disclosure nor direct access to accounts.

Send requests to the contact point above, with the subject “Authorities — Histae” and the case reference. This address is not an emergency service; no round-the-clock staffing or guaranteed response time is announced.

## 2. Required information

The request must allow identification and verification of:

- The authority, country, department, authorised officer's name and capacity, with a professional email address and an official callback method.
- The case number, purpose of the proceedings and legal basis relied upon, together with the applicable order or legally authorised demand.
- The accounts concerned, preferably by Histae UUID; any other identifier must be precise and justified.
- The data categories requested, their relevance to the proceedings and the targeted period, with dates, times and time zone.
- Statutory deadlines, the requested response method and any order imposing confidentiality or prohibiting notification of the person.

Do not transmit passwords, SMS codes or payment card details. Particularly sensitive content requires prior agreement on an appropriate transmission channel; do not spontaneously email images of child sexual abuse.

## 3. Verification and disclosure

The authorised responsible person must verify the requester's authenticity using independently obtained official contact details, their authority, the validity of the legal instrument, and the necessity and proportionality of its scope. An incomplete, unauthenticated or overly broad request may require clarification or be challenged where the law allows.

Disclosure may occur only on an applicable legal basis and only for necessary, available data covered by that basis, taking any sensitive nature into account. An email alone does not authorise disclosure. Foreign requests are assessed against applicable law, required judicial assistance mechanisms and data transfer rules; a foreign legal instrument does not automatically create an obligation to disclose.

Responses must use a channel appropriate to the sensitivity of the data and be limited to authorised recipients. Histae provides no permanent or general access to accounts. Requests from individuals or private disputes do not provide access to other people's data through this policy.

## 4. Preservation and availability

A preservation request must specify its legal basis, targeted accounts and categories, period, required preservation duration and case reference. Targeted preservation, when legally required and technically feasible, must be confirmed with its scope and duration; preservation alone does not authorise subsequent disclosure.

Privacy Policy deletion periods continue to run. Sending a request does not automatically suspend purges or guarantee preservation without confirmation. Histae does not promise recovery of already deleted data or provision of data never collected. A request does not create a location history or real-time surveillance capability.

## 5. Emergencies and child protection

In immediate danger, contact the competent emergency services first. An authority may report an emergency to the contact provided, with the subject “URGENT — immediate danger”, the facts establishing the risk, the people and accounts concerned, the strictly necessary data and callback details. Urgency does not remove the need to verify the authority or assess the legal basis; no automatic disclosure is promised.

Child sexual abuse or exploitation situations also follow the [Child Safety Standards](/en/child-safety/), including reporting confirmed cases to competent authorities in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Records, notification and review

The procedure provides for a limited record of the request, verification, legal basis, decision and information disclosed, with access restricted to authorised people and justified retention. Notification of the person concerned is assessed against applicable obligations and legal restrictions, without promising notification where the law prohibits it.

The legal identity, internal preservation and secure transmission arrangements, and final review of this policy remain in progress. This text describes the framework for manually handling requests; it does not attest to continuous staffing or an automated system.
