## 1. Scope and minimum age

This policy supplements the [Terms of Use](/en/legal/terms-of-use/) and applies to every account, free or paid, profiles, photos, messages and shared links. Histae is a dating service for adults, not a platform for selling sexual content or services. You must be at least 18 to use it. Falsifying your age, creating an account for a minor or allowing a minor to use your account is prohibited.

## 2. Prohibited content and use

You must not publish, transmit, request, promote or facilitate:

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation, related material (CSAM), sexual solicitation of a minor, grooming or sexual extortion. This includes artificially generated or manipulated depictions and links facilitating these acts.
- Human trafficking, sexual exploitation, prostitution, escort services, paid sexual encounters and the sale or promotion of sexual content or services.
- Pornographic content, images of nudity or sexual acts, including those involving consenting adults.
- Intimate images shared without permission, non-consensual sexual manipulations, disclosure of private data, stalking or threats to distribute such material.
- Harassment, threats, violence, incitement to hatred, discrimination or intimidation.
- Romance scams, fraudulent requests for money, phishing, impersonation, deceptive profiles or falsified documents.
- Spam, unauthorised commercial solicitation, content infringing others' rights, malware and unlawful activities.
- Unauthorised automated collection of profiles or data, access to other people's accounts, circumvention of safeguards, blocks or suspensions, and abusive use of automated tools.

## 3. Consent and respect for others

A match, a Premium subscription or agreement to reveal a photo does not entitle anyone to impose an interaction or redistribute another person's content. Respect refusals and expressed boundaries. Publish only content you are entitled to use, concerning you and complying with this policy. Agreement between two people does not make content prohibited here acceptable.

## 4. Reporting and child safety

Use the report-person option in a profile or conversation menu. For a suspected minor or an issue not listed, select the other-reason option and describe the facts. You can also email the contact above, even without account access, providing useful identifiers and dates. Do not attach sexual material involving a minor, download it or forward it.

The [Child Safety Standards](/en/child-safety/) describe the prohibitions, priority handling and referral to competent authorities. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services without waiting for a reply from Histae.

## 5. Moderation, measures and appeals

Histae may examine content and reports and, depending on the facts, remove content, restrict interactions, suspend or close an account. Content being available does not guarantee that it has been reviewed beforehand. Measures take account of seriousness, people's safety and applicable obligations. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.

To request an explanation or challenge a measure, write to the contact provided, identifying the account and decision concerned. A suspension does not remove mandatory rights relating to purchases or data protection. Reports and requests from authorities are handled under the [Law Enforcement Policy](/en/legal/law-enforcement-policy/) and [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy-policy/).
