# Consent to the processing of sensitive data

**Published version:** Validation in progress.  
**Data controller:** Validation in progress.

## What you agree to

Histae invites you to provide your declared sex and the people you wish to meet. In the context of a dating app, this information may reveal aspects of your sex life or sexual orientation. Its processing for this purpose relies on **separate, explicit consent**.

If you agree, Histae uses this information to apply your discovery criteria and those of other people in both directions. Your declared sex may be visible in your suggested profile; search preferences are used for filtering. Profile content you choose to publish may also reveal intimate information: avoid including data you do not wish to share with other users.

You may refuse. Your account may remain open, but discovery and the features that require these criteria will not be available until the required information and consent are present. No consent relating to sensitive data is inferred from acceptance of the Terms of Use or simply reading the Privacy Policy.

## Withdrawal and retention

You may withdraw this consent in the app's consent settings. The API then deletes the sex and preferences concerned and stops using them for new discoveries. Withdrawal does not retroactively make information already received, copied or captured by other users invisible. Evidence of choices and withdrawals may be retained for the period indicated in the Privacy Policy.

For recipients, retention periods and the exercise of your rights, see the Privacy Policy. Questions: Validation in progress.
