## What you agree to

When you activate discovery, the app transmits latitude and longitude to Histae. Before recording presence, the server replaces them with the centre of an approximate zone of about 5 km. Discovery uses these centres and mutual distance preferences; its thresholds are approximate. Other users see a distance rounded in 5 km steps, with a minimum of 5 km, not your precise coordinates. This reduces precision without guaranteeing anonymity or proving your actual location. Changing zones is limited to once every five minutes; updates within the same zone remain possible.

Presence retains only the latest zone centre and update date, not a travel history. It can no longer be used for discovery after one hour without an update; maintenance purges it after 24 hours. You may refuse or withdraw your agreement: withdrawal deletes recorded presence and prevents dependent features until renewed consent and a new location are provided. Data already received by others and backups are subject to the Privacy Policy's limitations and retention periods.

The location permission granted in your phone's settings is separate from this consent. You can change it in your operating system; the app must also collect your choice through its own process. This agreement does not cover any advertising use of the location.

For recipients, your rights and other retention periods, see the Privacy Policy. Questions: [support.histae@gmail.com](mailto:support.histae@gmail.com).
