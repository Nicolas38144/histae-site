## What you agree to

When you activate discovery, Histae may receive the location transmitted by the app as latitude and longitude. The location is used to calculate distances between profiles and apply the distance limits chosen by both people. Other users see a rounded distance, never your precise coordinates.

The API retains the last location transmitted, rather than a travel history. This location can no longer be used for discovery after one hour; without an update, it is deleted after 24 hours. You may refuse or withdraw your agreement. Withdrawal deletes the recorded location and stops distance-based discovery until renewed agreement and a new location are provided.

The location permission granted in your phone's settings is separate from this consent. You can change it in your operating system; the app must also collect your choice through its own process. This agreement does not cover any advertising use of the location.
